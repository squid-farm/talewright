# Where Talewright is headed

Talewright already works. You can write a story, play it, save your progress, check for mistakes, debug it
and publish it for the web or desktop.

Right now, I'm writing a full story with it. That's the real test: if something gets in my way, it gets
fixed before I call Talewright ready for anyone else.

This is a direction, not a schedule. Day-to-day progress is on the
[project board](https://github.com/orgs/squid-farm/projects/1).

## Now: getting to the first 1.0 preview

- **A better debugger.** A redesigned way to test a story, check the paths through it and see what happened
  along the way.
- **What my own story needs.** Writing a real story is turning up gaps. Fixing them comes first.
- **A story language you can rely on.** Once the preview ships, stories you write won't break in later 1.x
  releases.

## In progress

- **Reading your way.** Players can already adjust parts of the screen. Next comes more control over layout,
  fonts and how much game detail appears.
- **Fixing mistakes for you.** The VS Code extension can already fix some mistakes in one click. More fixes
  are on the way.

## Later

- **Stories with their own look.** Themes, fonts, speaker styles and custom end screens.
- **Living worlds.** Places, travel, time and events that move on as the player acts.
- **Saves that survive story updates,** even after scenes are changed or renamed.
- **Optional combat** for stories that want it.
- **Plugins** for things Talewright doesn't handle on its own.

## Someday, if people want it

**One place to make a story.** Write, preview, test and explore the story map in a single app.

For now, the goal is simple: get Talewright to a point I'm happy with, then find out whether other people
are happy with it too.

Have a story Talewright can't tell yet? [Open an issue](https://github.com/squid-farm/talewright/issues).
